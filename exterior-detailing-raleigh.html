What is an exterior detail?

An exterior detail is a complete outside reset — not a quick rinse. We strip away bonded contamination, restore gloss and lay down protection so the paint, wheels and glass all look their best and stay cleaner for longer.

  • Truly clean paintFoam hand wash, then iron, tar and clay decontamination to remove what a normal wash leaves behind.
  • Wheels, tires & glassDeep-cleaned wheels and wells, dressed tires, streak-free glass and restored exterior trim.
  • Gloss & protectionA gloss-enhancing polish and a wax or sealant that deepen the shine and shield the finish.

Why book an exterior detail?

More than a clean car — a protected, glossier finish that holds up to Carolina roads and weather.

  • Deep, glossy shine

    Decontamination and polish bring back a wet, reflective finish a wash can't.

  • Blocks sun fade

    Wax or sealant shields paint from the Carolina UV rays that dull clear coat.

  • Repels water & grime

    Fresh protection sheets off rain and road film, so it stays cleaner longer.

  • Spotless wheels

    Deep-cleaned wheels, wells and dressed tires make the whole car look new.

  • Removes contaminants

    Lifts bird droppings, sap, bugs and iron fallout before they damage paint.

How we exterior-detail your car

A careful, by-hand process that cleans, decontaminates, restores gloss and protects — never a rushed drive-through wash.

1
Step 01

Wheels & Foam Wash

We start with wheels, tires and wells, then a foam pre-soak and gentle hand wash to lift dirt safely.

2
Step 02

Decontamination

Iron remover, tar removal and a clay treatment strip bonded contaminants a wash leaves behind.

3
Step 03

Gloss Enhancement

A gloss-enhancing polish or wax deepens shine, then we dress tires and trim and clean the glass.

4
Step 04

Protect & Inspect

We seal the paint with wax or sealant and inspect under lighting to confirm a flawless, glossy finish.

Exterior detailing, explained

An exterior detail covers everything outside the vehicle — a foam hand wash, wheel and tire deep clean, iron and tar decontamination, bug and grime removal, a gloss-enhancing polish or wax, plus clean glass and dressed trim. The result is a bright, glossy, protected finish.

Our exterior detailing ranges from about $150 for an express service up to $425 for a full show-level exterior with decontamination and polish. Pricing depends on the size and condition of your vehicle. Every quote is tailored to your car.

A wash removes loose dirt. An exterior detail goes much further — decontaminating bonded fallout, clay-treating the paint, polishing for gloss and adding protection. It restores and protects the finish rather than just cleaning the surface.

Yes. Every exterior detail includes a thorough wheel and tire clean, tire dressing, streak-free glass and dressed exterior trim. Wheels and tires are one of the most impactful parts of a clean exterior, so we never skip them.

Yes. Tang Detailing is fully mobile — we bring our own water, power and equipment to your home or office across Raleigh and the greater Triangle. We just need a bit of space around the vehicle to work.

An exterior detail cleans, polishes and protects your paint with a wax or sealant that lasts weeks to months. A ceramic coating is a longer-term protection lasting years. Many clients start with an exterior detail and upgrade to a ceramic coating for lasting protection.
