Full Interior & Exterior Detail

A deep clean inside and out for a busy family SUV.

VehicleFamily SUV
ServiceFull Detail
LocationRaleigh, NC

The project

Family life is tough on a vehicle — crumbs in the seats, dust on every surface and road film on the paint. This SUV came in needing a true reset, so we booked a full interior and exterior detail to bring back that fresh, like-new feel from bumper to bumper.

Inside, we vacuumed, steam-cleaned and dressed every surface. Outside, a thorough hand wash and decontamination lifted months of built-up grime, and a spray sealant left the paint slick and glossy. The owner got back a car that looks and smells brand new.

What we did

  • Full interior cleanVacuum and steam clean of seats, carpets and mats, plus wiped-down panels.
  • Surfaces dressedDash, console, door panels and trim cleaned and given a clean, non-greasy finish.
  • Exterior hand washFoam wash, wheels and tires cleaned, plus decontamination of bonded grime.
  • Seal & finishSpray sealant for gloss and protection, glass cleaned and tires dressed.
