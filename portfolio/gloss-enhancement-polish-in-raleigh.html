ServiceSingle-Stage Polish

The project

Not every car needs a full multi-stage correction. This sedan's paint was in good shape but had lost some of its depth and pop, with a few light swirls dulling the reflections. A gloss enhancement polish was the perfect middle ground — a single machine pass that maximizes shine and removes the lightest defects.

We washed and decontaminated the paint, then used a fine polish to clean up the finish and restore a wet, glassy look. It's a fast, cost-effective way to make a well-kept car look its absolute best.

What we did

  • Wash & decontaminationpH-neutral wash and clay to remove bonded contaminants before polishing.
  • Single-stage polishOne refined machine pass to boost clarity, depth and gloss.
  • Light defect removalKnocked back the finest swirls and haze for cleaner reflections.
  • Seal & finishProtected the paint and detailed trim, glass and wheels to finish.
