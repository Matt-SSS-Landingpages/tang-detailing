The project

This 2019 Range Rover was a brand-new purchase, and its owner was thrilled about it — he wanted our detailing to be the finishing touch that made the truck truly his own. The paint told a different story up close, though: heavy swirling and oxidation from sun exposure and repeated automatic car washes had dulled that deep black finish.

Automatic car washes and constant sun are hard on dark paint — stiff brushes drag grit across the surface and etch in swirl marks, while UV slowly oxidizes the clear coat. We started by claying and fully decontaminating the paint, then ran a two-step correction: a cutting stage to slice through the swirls and oxidation, followed by a refining polish to bring back clarity and depth. With the finish restored, we locked it in with a 7-year ceramic coating across every painted surface, the glass, and the wheels — so the gloss lasts and future washes stay gentle on it.

What we did

  • Clay bar & full decontaminationA clay bar treatment and complete paint decontamination to lift bonded contaminants before polishing.
  • Two-step paint correctionA cutting stage to erase the swirls and oxidation, followed by a refining polish to restore clarity and deep black depth.
  • 7-year ceramic coating — paint, glass & wheelsA durable 7-year ceramic coating applied to all painted surfaces, the glass, and the wheels for lasting gloss and protection.
  • Final inspectionThe whole vehicle inspected under light to confirm an even, deep finish before handing back the keys.
