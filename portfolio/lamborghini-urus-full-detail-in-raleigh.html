The project

This 2024 Lamborghini Urus came in wearing normal road grime after a lot of long-distance driving between North Carolina and New York. Its owner trusted us with something most shops are wary of: an exotic finished in delicate satin (matte) paint.

Satin and matte finishes are their own discipline. They can't be washed or worked like ordinary glossy paint — the wrong products or the wrong technique can leave permanent shiny spots that ruin the look. Working safely on that finish was the owner's main concern, and it's exactly where we're at home: matte-safe products and a careful, deliberate hand process from start to finish. We gave the Urus a complete detail inside and out — a full interior deep clean and surface protection, a gentle foam hand wash, and the wheels cleaned thoroughly all around.

What we did

  • Full interior deep cleanAir blowout and vacuum throughout, with every crack and crevice detailed by hand.
  • Interior surface protectionA protective dressing applied to interior surfaces to keep them looking and feeling factory-fresh.
  • Satin-safe foam hand washA gentle foam bath and careful hand wash using products formulated specifically for delicate matte and satin paint.
  • Wheels fully cleanedWheels cleaned thoroughly all around, then the whole vehicle inspected before handing back the keys.
