Wheel & Exterior Detail

Spotless wheels and a slick, glossy finish.

VehiclePerformance Coupe
ServiceExterior Detail
LocationRaleigh, NC

The project

Wheels take the worst of the road — brake dust, tar and baked-on grime that a normal wash never fully removes. This performance coupe's owner wanted the wheels and paint brought back to a showroom shine, so we focused an exterior detail on the details that make a car pop.

We deep-cleaned each wheel face and barrel, then moved to a full decontamination wash of the paint. A spray sealant finished the job for a slick, water-beading surface and crisp, mirror-like reflections.

What we did

  • Deep wheel cleanFaces, barrels and lug areas cleaned of brake dust, tar and road grime.
  • Decontamination washFoam wash, iron removal and clay to strip bonded contaminants from the paint.
  • Seal for glossSpray sealant applied for a slick, water-beading, high-gloss finish.
  • Tires, trim & glassTires dressed, trim refreshed and glass cleaned for a complete finish.
