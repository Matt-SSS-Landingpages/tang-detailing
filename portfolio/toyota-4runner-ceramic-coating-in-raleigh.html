Toyota 4Runner 7-Year Ceramic Coating

Factory-fresh protection, locked in from day one — Raleigh, NC.

Vehicle2026 Toyota 4Runner Limited i-FORCE MAX
Service7-Year Ceramic Coating
LocationRaleigh, NC

The project

This 2026 Toyota 4Runner Limited i-FORCE MAX rolled in brand-new — factory paint, not a swirl on it. Its owner wanted that flawless finish protected the right way from day one, before daily driving, car washes and Carolina sun ever had a chance to take their toll.

Starting protection early is the smart move on a new vehicle: the paint is already in perfect condition, so there's nothing to correct — only to preserve. The owner brought it to a dedicated coating specialist to lock in years of gloss and defense while the finish was still showroom-perfect. We prepped the paint meticulously, then bonded our 7-year ceramic coating to every painted surface, the glass and the wheels for long-term, easy-to-maintain protection.

What we did

  • Clay bar & full decontaminationClay treatment and a complete paint decontamination to leave every panel perfectly clean and glass-smooth.
  • 7-year ceramic coatingOur professional-grade 7-year coating applied by hand to all painted surfaces for years of gloss and protection.
  • Glass & wheels coatedCoating extended to the glass and wheels for hydrophobic, easier-to-clean surfaces all around.
  • Final inspectionEvery surface checked under lighting to confirm an even, flawless finish before delivery.
