Got Questions?

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How long does the ceramic coating process take?
The full process typically takes 2–3 days. This includes thorough decontamination, paint correction, and the coating application itself. Rushing this process compromises the bond — Tang doesn't cut corners.
How is ceramic coating different from wax or sealant?
Wax and sealant sit on top of your paint and wear off within weeks to months. Ceramic coating chemically bonds to your clear coat, creating a semi-permanent layer of protection that lasts years — not weeks.
How do I maintain my car after ceramic coating?
Maintenance is minimal — that's the point. A simple hand wash every 2–4 weeks is all it takes. Avoid automatic car washes with harsh brushes. Tang walks you through everything at pickup so you know exactly what to do.
Is my car a good candidate for ceramic coating?
Most vehicles are great candidates — from luxury cars to high-end trucks. Tang will assess your paint condition during the quote process and let you know if any correction is needed before coating. The better the prep, the better the result.
What does the 6-month warranty check-in include?
Six months after your coating, Tang personally comes back to inspect adhesion, gloss retention, and hydrophobic performance. If anything isn't performing as expected, he addresses it on the spot. This isn't a phone call — it's a physical inspection.
Do you service the entire Raleigh area?
Yes — Tang services Raleigh and the surrounding areas of the Triangle region. Reach out via the quote form and he'll confirm whether your location is covered.
