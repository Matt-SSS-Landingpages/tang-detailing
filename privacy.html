Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

Tang Detailing ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit tangdetailing.com or request a quote, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us, along with basic technical data your browser shares automatically. This includes:

  • Contact details you submit through our quote or contact forms — name, phone number, email address and vehicle or service details.
  • Communications you send us by phone, text, email or social media.
  • Usage data such as your IP address, device type, browser and the pages you view, collected through cookies and analytics tools.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide quotes.
  • Schedule, perform and follow up on detailing services.
  • Send appointment reminders and service-related messages.
  • Improve our website, services and marketing.

We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies & analytics

Our site uses cookies and third-party analytics (including Google Analytics via Google Tag Manager) to understand how visitors use the site and to measure the performance of our advertising. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work as intended.

Sharing your information

We share information only with service providers who help us operate our business — for example, scheduling, payment, hosting and analytics vendors — and only to the extent needed to perform those services. We may also disclose information when required by law.

Data security & retention

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. We keep your information only as long as needed to provide our services and meet legal or accounting requirements.

Your choices

You may request that we access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or opt out of marketing messages, by contacting us using the details below.
