What is paint correction?

Not a cover-up. A true restoration.

A wax or glaze temporarily hides swirl marks. Paint correction is different — we carefully machine-polish the clear coat to physically remove defects, so the gloss you see is the paint itself at its absolute best.

  • Removes defects permanentlySwirls, buffer trails, water spots and oxidation are polished out — not filled in.
  • Restores depth & clarityLeveling the paint brings back reflections, richness and a true wet look.
  • The perfect base for coatingCorrection is the ideal first step before a ceramic coating locks the finish in.
What we fix

Say goodbye to tired paint

If your paint looks hazy in the sun or covered in fine spider-web scratches, correction is the answer. Here's what we remove.

Swirl Marks & Spider-Webbing

The fine circular scratches from automatic car washes and improper drying that dull your paint in direct sunlight.

Light Scratches

Surface scratches and scuffs that haven't gone through the clear coat can be leveled and polished away.

Water Spots & Etching

Mineral deposits and hard-water etching that leave rings and blotches baked into the finish.

Oxidation & Dullness

Sun-faded, chalky and hazy paint restored to rich, saturated color and clarity.

Buffer Trails & Holograms

The hazy streaks left behind by rushed, low-quality polishing jobs — corrected the right way.

Lost Gloss & Reflection

Bring back the deep, mirror-like reflections your paint had the day it left the showroom.

Our process

How correction works

Every job is measured and tailored to your paint. We use the least aggressive method that safely delivers flawless results.

1

Wash & Decontaminate

A safe hand wash, iron removal and clay treatment lift embedded grime so we polish clean, bare paint.

2

Inspect & Measure

Under bright inspection lighting we assess defects and measure paint thickness to build a safe plan.

3

Compound & Refine

Machine compounding removes the deeper defects, then refining polishes bring out maximum gloss.

4

Protect the Finish

We seal or coat the fresh paint so all that restored clarity and gloss is locked in and protected.

FAQ

Paint correction, explained

Paint correction is a multi-stage machine polishing process that permanently removes swirl marks, scratches, water spots and oxidation from your clear coat. Unlike a wax or glaze that hides defects, correction actually levels the paint to restore true depth, clarity and gloss.

Most light-to-moderate scratches and swirl marks can be dramatically reduced or fully removed with professional paint correction. Deeper scratches that have gone through the clear coat can usually be improved but not completely erased without repainting.

It depends on the level of correction and the condition of your paint. A one-step enhancement often takes a full day, while multi-stage correction on heavily swirled paint can take two or more days. We never rush the process.

A one-step (or enhancement) polish removes roughly 60–70% of defects and boosts gloss in a single pass — great value for newer or lightly marred cars. Multi-stage correction uses compounding plus refining steps to remove 85–95%-plus of defects for near-flawless, show-quality results.
