Questions, answered

Everything you need to know about mobile detailing and ceramic coating in Durham.

Yes. Tang Detailing is a fully mobile service. We bring our own water, power and professional equipment to your home or office anywhere in Durham and the greater Triangle area.

A standard detail takes 3–4 hours, while a full detail or paint correction can run 4–8 hours depending on vehicle size and condition. We never rush a job.

Standard service ranges $240–$290, full details $340–$390, exterior details from $150, and interior packages from $150. Ceramic coating starts at $799.

Ceramic coating is a liquid polymer that chemically bonds to your paint, creating a durable, hydrophobic layer of protection. Professional coatings from Tang Detailing last 2–7 years depending on the package.

Not necessarily. As long as we have access to your vehicle and a place to work, you're free to go about your day. Many clients leave us to it while they work or run errands.

We serve Raleigh, Cary, Durham, Wake Forest, Apex and Chapel Hill, NC, plus surrounding Triangle communities.

We use only professional-grade products from Gyeon, CarPro, Gtechniq, Rupes and P&S Detail Products — never cheap consumer chemicals.

Yes, we are fully insured and every service is backed by our satisfaction guarantee.

Paint correction is a multi-stage machine polishing process that removes swirl marks, scratches and oxidation to restore a deep, glossy finish before coating.

For most drivers, a full detail every 3–4 months keeps a vehicle in top shape. Our Maintenance Club members receive scheduled visits to keep protection at its peak.

Most light-to-moderate scratches and swirl marks can be dramatically reduced or removed with professional paint correction. Deeper scratches through the clear coat may only be improved.
